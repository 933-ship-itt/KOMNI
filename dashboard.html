02 / WHAT IS THE POLICY ENGINE?

Think of it as the layer that decides what happens next.

Business transactions can pass through KOMNI before continuing to their destination.

The Policy Engine is where those decisions will eventually become configurable rules.

×
IMPORTANT KOMNI is not a payment processor.
03 / KNOW THE DIFFERENCE

KOMNI does not replace your payment provider.

You still use a processor such as Stripe to actually process payments.

KOMNI is the middleware layer around that infrastructure.
